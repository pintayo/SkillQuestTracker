# SkillQuest Tracker - App Guide

## 🎯 Your Instagram Content Strategy

This app is built specifically for your **"Learning Skills" Instagram journey**. Here's how to use it:

### 📱 App Features

#### 1. **Home Screen** (Perfect for Instagram Stories)
- Shows your "Today's Quest" - the skill you should practice
- Big "START NOW" button - no decisions needed
- Progress bars showing hours logged
- Stats: Total hours + unlocked skills

**Instagram Tip:** Screenshot this screen at the start of each video!

#### 2. **Session Timer** (While You Practice)
- Simple timer that counts your practice time
- Add notes after each session
- Shows your progress in real-time

**Instagram Tip:** Quick cut to this screen during your videos to show you're tracking!

#### 3. **Skill Tree** 🌳 (The Money Shot!)
- Visual "reverse family tree" of all your skills
- Foundation skills at the bottom unlock advanced skills above
- Circles show progress percentages
- Locked skills shown with 🔒 until you complete prerequisites

**Instagram Tip:** This is your best visual content! Show it at the end of videos:
- "Day 10 Skateboarding - 12 hours logged, 24% progress!"
- "Just unlocked Surfing after completing 50 hours of skateboarding!"

## 📲 App Usage

### First Time Setup
1. Open app - skills auto-initialize
2. See recommended skill (Skateboarding)
3. Tap "START NOW"
4. Practice
5. Log your session

### Daily Routine
1. **Morning:** Check app for today's quest
2. **Practice:** Do the skill (record for Instagram!)
3. **Log:** Open app, start timer, complete session
4. **Content:** Edit video, include app screenshots
5. **Post:** Share progress

### Weekly Routine
1. Review skill tree
2. Check progress percentages
3. Plan next week's content
4. See if any skills unlocked
